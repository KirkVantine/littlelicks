        The 1969 story

        This isn't a replica. It's a Chevrolet step-van that left the factory in 1969 wearing Good Humor white, built to sell ice cream bars one street at a time.

        1. 1920Harry Burt of Youngstown, Ohio puts a chocolate-coated ice cream bar on a stick and sends it out in white trucks with bells. Good Humor is born.
        2. 1969This truck rolls out as part of the Good Humor fleet, bells, freezer box and all.
        3. 1976Good Humor moves to grocery stores and sells off its trucks. Most of the fleet disappears. A few survive in private hands.
        4. 2025Heather brings this one to Chelsea, paints the roof red, and starts Little Licks Tasty Treats.
