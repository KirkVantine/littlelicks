# A website for Little Licks Tasty Treats

Hi Heather,

I put together a working preview of what a website for the truck could look like. It's live here, and it works on your phone:

**https://kirkvantine.github.io/littlelicks/**

Everything on it came from your Facebook page: the logo, the truck's red, white and blue, your photos, and the stops you've posted this season.

## What's on the site

| Section | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| The truck, front and center | An illustrated version of your truck drives in when the page opens, the window pops up, and visitors can ring the bell. |
| Find the truck | Your public stops in date order, with today's stop called out at the top of the page. Past stops tuck away under "Where the truck has been this season." |
| Fresh from Facebook | Your live Facebook feed, right on the page. Keep posting the way you do now and the site shows it automatically. |
| On the truck | Dixboro Dairy scoops, Lake House Bakery sandwiches, the classics, and the gluten-free, dairy-free and vegan options. |
| The 1969 story | The truck's Good Humor history, from 1920 to you. |
| Photos | A gallery of the truck and your events, with a filter and a full-screen viewer. |
| Book the truck | A request form that collects the date, place, kind of event, guest count and who's buying, then sends you a tidy, numbered request by email. Your phone number is right beside it for people who'd rather call or text. |

## What I'd need from you

1. **The truck's real story.** I wrote the history section from what's public about Good Humor and from your posts. Where the truck spent its years before you, how you found it, and what the restoration took would make that section yours. Please correct anything I got wrong, including the 2025 start date.
2. **Photos you'd like featured.** The preview uses photos from your Facebook page. I left out the pictures of kids until you say which ones are okay to show, and I left out the Gary DeLisle car show photo until we have his permission.
3. **Upcoming stops.** Everything on your page right now has already happened or is happening today, so the "Coming up" list is short. Send me dates as you book them, or I can show you how to add one yourself. It's one line per stop.
4. **A web address.** Something like `littlelickstastytreats.com`. Registration is included, so we just need to pick one together.
